আব্বু, এই পাতাটি প্রিন্ট করে Mac-এর পাশে রাখুন। দেখে দেখে কাজ করুন।

The Big Five

⌘+C
Copy
⌘+V
Paste
⌘+X
Cut
⌘+Z
Undo (panic button)
⌘+S
Save

Find Anything

⌘+Space
Spotlight, search the whole Mac
⌘+F
Find on this page
⌘+Tab
Switch between open apps
Space
Quick Look, preview any file

Windows & Apps

⌘+N
New window or document
⌘+T
New tab in browser
⌘+W
Close window or tab
⌘+Q
Quit the app
⌘+M
Minimize to Dock
⌘+H
Hide app

If Things Go Wrong

⌘+Z
Undo whatever just happened
⌘+⌥+Esc
Force Quit a frozen app
⌘+Shift+T
Reopen closed browser tab
⌘+Shift+Z
Redo (undo your undo)

Screenshots

⌘+Shift+3
Whole screen
⌘+Shift+4
Drag to choose area
⌘+Shift+5
Screenshot menu (record video)
Screenshots save to your Desktop.

Files & Finder

⌘+N
New Finder window
⌘+Delete
Move to Trash
⌘+Shift+D
Jump to Desktop
⌘+⌥+L
Jump to Downloads
⌘+Shift+O
Jump to Documents
⌘+Shift+H
Jump to Home folder

Where Things Live

Downloaded files
Finder → Downloads
Screenshots
Desktop
Saved Word/Excel
Wherever you chose, usually Documents
Trash
Bottom-right of Dock
All your apps
Finder → Applications

The Special Keys

⌘
Command, like Ctrl on Windows
⌥
Option, like Alt on Windows
⌃
Control, used in some shortcuts
⇧
Shift
fn
Function, bottom left of keyboard
